## Best use case

Choose `colorize` when the layout is serviceable but the atmosphere and emphasis still feel underpowered.

## What it should change

The command should introduce a more deliberate palette and clearer accent logic, not just sprinkle random color on neutral UI.

## Success condition

The interface should feel more intentional while staying readable and controlled.
