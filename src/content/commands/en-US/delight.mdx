## When to use it

Reach for `delight` after the fundamentals are stable. It is most effective as an enhancement layer, not a rescue plan.

## What it can improve

Expect playful copy moments, micro-interactions, and visual cues that make the surface feel more human and intentional.

## Quality bar

The result should be distinctive, not noisy.
