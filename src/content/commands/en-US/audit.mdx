## When to use it

Choose `audit` when the work already exists and you need a defensible quality review grounded in checks rather than preference.

## What it surfaces

The command should call out concrete risks such as contrast failures, layout regressions, poor scaling, and performance or theming gaps.

## Expected next step

Findings usually feed into `harden`, `optimize`, `adapt`, or `clarify`.
