## When to use it

Choose `live` when the most useful feedback loop is inside the browser, with the actual UI and actual constraints on screen.

## What it enables

The command should help generate and inspect variants quickly, then accept the direction that best fits the brief.

## Strength

It is strongest as an iteration surface after a direction already exists.
