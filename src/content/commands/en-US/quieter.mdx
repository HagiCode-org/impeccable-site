## Best use case

Choose `quieter` when the work has energy already and needs editing rather than amplification.

## Likely results

The command should reduce excess contrast, clutter, and competing emphasis while preserving the intent of the surface.

## Success condition

The page should feel calmer and clearer, not merely emptier.
