## When to use it

Choose `adapt` when the same experience needs to survive across viewports or platforms and simple breakpoint tweaks are no longer enough.

## Areas of focus

The command should address hierarchy, controls, density, and content order for the target context.

## Success condition

The adapted version should still feel like the same product, just tuned to a different environment.
