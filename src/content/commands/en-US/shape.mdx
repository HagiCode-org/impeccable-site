## When to use it

Choose `shape` when the missing piece is thinking, not code. It is the right command for planning a surface, a flow, or a significant redesign.

## What to expect

The command should gather context about purpose, users, content, constraints, and visual direction, then convert that into a concrete brief.

## Output quality bar

The result should be specific enough that another implementation step can trace decisions back to the brief.
