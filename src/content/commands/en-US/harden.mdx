## Best fit

Use `harden` when the work exists, but confidence in real-world behavior is still low.

## Areas it should cover

Expect attention on edge states, loading, empty content, text overflow, and other reliability gaps that show up outside demos.

## Success condition

The feature should still read clearly when data, locale, or network conditions are messy.
