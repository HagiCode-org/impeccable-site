## Best fit

Use `bolder` when the design is functional but timid. It is about stronger choices, not random embellishment.

## Common shifts

Expect firmer hierarchy, more committed type or color, and a clearer visual center of gravity.

## Counterbalance

If the work crosses the line into aggression, follow with `quieter` or `distill`.
