## When to use it

Reach for `optimize` when quality problems are measurable in speed, responsiveness, or asset weight rather than in taste alone.

## Likely interventions

The command may reduce expensive effects, improve loading behavior, or simplify rendering paths while protecting the visual intent.

## Goal

The interface should feel faster without feeling stripped down.
