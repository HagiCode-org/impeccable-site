## Best fit

Use `critique` after something exists on screen. It is strongest when the question is "what is not landing yet?"

## Review focus

Expect attention on hierarchy, cognitive load, emotional tone, and whether the page feels coherent for the intended user.

## Follow-through

The strongest runs turn directly into a narrower fix pass such as `polish`, `distill`, `layout`, or `typeset`.
