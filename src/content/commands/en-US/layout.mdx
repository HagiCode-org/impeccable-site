## When it helps most

Use `layout` when the structure is doing most of the damage: weak grouping, repetitive card grids, uneven density, or poor pacing.

## Likely outcomes

Expect changes to hierarchy, blocks, spacing rhythm, and the balance between scanning and depth.

## What not to expect

This command is not mainly about polish details; it is about spatial strategy.
