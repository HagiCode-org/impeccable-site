## Best fit

Use `typeset` when the page is text-heavy or when typography is the clearest bottleneck to perceived quality.

## Typical improvements

Good runs tighten scale, weight, spacing, line length, and font pairing so the interface starts to feel authored.

## Guardrail

The goal is not novelty for its own sake; it is legibility plus intent.
