## When it earns its keep

Use `overdrive` for hero moments, showcases, or interactions where technical ambition is itself part of the value.

## Trade-off

This command intentionally raises complexity. It should be used where that cost is justified.

## Quality bar

The result should still feel designed, not like an effects demo stapled to a product page.
