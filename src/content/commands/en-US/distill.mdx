## Best fit

Choose `distill` when subtraction is the real answer. It helps most when the problem is accumulation, not absence.

## What it should remove

Expect weak ornaments, redundant content, unnecessary states, and extra visual noise to be challenged.

## Result

The page should feel more focused and more readable, not under-designed.
